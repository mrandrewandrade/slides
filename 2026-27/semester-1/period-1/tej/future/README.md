# TEJ Future lessons

This directory contains staged TEJ lesson decks that are not Current and are not yet part of Full.

## Lifecycle

1. Develop and render the staged source here.
2. Review projector layout, links, accessibility, and source alignment.
3. Do not move or include it in `current/tej.qmd` until the teacher explicitly authorizes promotion.
4. Do not add it to `tej/full.qmd` until the lesson has been completed and the normal advance workflow is authorized.

The H01 electronics deck is intentionally staged only.
