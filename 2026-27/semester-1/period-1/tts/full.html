About Me - Purpose

Help us get to know you.
Help us connect with you.

Guideline: about 1-3 minutes.

What could you share?

  • skills
  • hobbies/interests
  • something you made, fixed, designed, or worked on
  • something you are curious about
  • future goals or something you want to learn, make, fix, or try
  • a fun fact

You do not need to include everything.

Keep It Genuine

You do not need to be outgoing, funny, artistic, or have an impressive list of accomplishments.

A simple, clear, genuine presentation can earn 100%.

If You Use Slides

  • large readable text
  • clear contrast
  • simple layouts
  • useful images
  • not too much text
  • do not rely on colour alone

Fancy slides are not the goal. Clear, accessible communication is.

Arrive Every Day On Time

Be Present

If you can’t be on time, be ahead of time.

I close the door when the bell rings so we do not disturb the opening activity or disrupt the peace.

  • Sit down. Relax.
  • Look at the quote.
  • Phones are okay until attendance is finished.
  • No talking during attendance.

Why do this?

Why do you think we start class this way?

Attendance

Physically present: required.

When called, say “here” loudly enough for me to hear.

If I do not hear you and see you, I cannot mark you present.

Attendance

Mentally, spiritually, and emotionally present: optional, but encouraged.

We are here to support you no matter what you are going through.

Participation

  • Participation includes in class and online.
  • Lateness, extended time out of class, and avoiding participation count.
  • Preventing someone else from learning also counts.

New Ministry rules: participation is a significant part of the grade.

Essential Expectations From You

  • Respect the timers and transitions.
  • Respect no-phone moments.
  • Respect no-talking moments.
  • Let other people learn.

Otherwise, I am very flexible. This class should be a lot of fun.

Exceeding Expectations

To get a Level 4 or above, make this class better.

Teach me something new.

Very Difficult to Fail

Submit your work and it is very difficult to fail my class.

The main way to fail is to submit little or nothing.

More importantly: do not prevent other people from learning.

Any Questions?

Ask now. Ask later. Ask when you need help.

If you think something can be better, speak up.
We want to grow together.

One PDF

One PDF. Submitted by: student-number and clear name tag photo.

Why So Specific?

  • The deliverable has to work for the person receiving it.
  • It must be professional and confidential.

Why PDF?

  • It looks the same for everyone.
  • Editable files are working files and can have hidden details. A PDF is the deliverable.

Example PDF

One page. Clear photo. Student number.

Open the example PDF

How I Am Marking It

  • Photo quality · /2
  • Crop + layout · /2
  • Student number · /2
  • PDF skill · /2
  • Following instructions · /2

/10 · Communication and process, not artistic talent.

Document + Submit

  1. Take a clear, well-lit photo.
  2. Put it on one page and crop it cleanly.
  3. Add Submitted by: your-student-number.
  4. Export as PDF.
  5. Open the PDF and check it.
  6. Submit it to Google Classroom.

Mission Complete

  • Name tag is easy to read.
  • Photo is clear and well composed.
  • Student number uses the required format.
  • PDF opens and looks correct.
  • Correct PDF is submitted.
  • Physical name tag goes home and comes back usable.
