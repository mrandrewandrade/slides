# Semester 1 Slide Design Direction

This branch carries the Semester 1 slide work through the exam period.

## Visual direction

- Keep opening quote slides extremely minimal and spacious.
- Use the Commons/AA branding sparingly, not as repeated decoration on every slide.
- Keep footer and navigation chrome subtle, using a medium grey with low visual emphasis.
- Prefer a Tufte-inspired approach: maximize information clarity, remove nonessential decoration, and use whitespace deliberately.
- Use Port Credit Navy and Technical Blue for structure and interaction, with restrained PCSS Gold accents.
- Reserve strong alert colours for genuinely important notices.
- Keep projector readability as the priority: large type, high contrast, short lines, and simple layouts.

## Content direction

- Weeks 1 and 2 contain real teaching content.
- Later Semester 1 dates remain calendar-complete and easy to edit.
- December introduces no major new content.
- The final two instructional days before winter break are community/game days.
- The first instructional day after winter break introduces culminating work.
- January supports culminating checkpoints and documentation.
- The exam period uses a calm written reflection and course-feedback deck.

## Design rule

Be rigid about the learning process and flexible about the technical content.
